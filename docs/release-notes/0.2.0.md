# Leftoff Agents 0.2.0

The PM now keeps track of what **you** have to do, not only what your agents do — and it bothers you less.

## Your own to-do list

- **«Your tasks» (D-070).** When an agent needs something only you can do — a command on a server, a key to add, a
  choice, a check on a phone — it becomes a task, `OWN-n`, tied to its project. Born from an agent's «Owner: …» note,
  its question, a handoff to you, the operational PM, or your own `/task`. The same thing twice is one task; likely
  duplicates are marked «≈ OWN-n» and can be merged.
- **A page of its own** in the panel: newest first, or oldest, or by project, each project with its colour; what
  **blocks the team** comes first, above urgent ones, and opens the 9:00 stand-up («The team is waiting for you on: …»).
- **Each task has its chat** with whoever helps — the operational PM by default, any agent you choose, or the PM.
  You see where your words went («delivered», «working on it…»), can ask again, and answers show within seconds. Replies render as Markdown, with code to copy.
- `/tasks`, `/done`, `/drop` in Telegram; `leftoff owner-tasks` and the MCP tool `get_owner_tasks` for agents.

## Attachments (D-071)

- Images and text files in a task's chat: pasted, dropped or picked, kept on your machine outside every repository,
  handed to the agent by their path. A long text is never cut: it becomes a Markdown file with its first lines shown.
- Text files are checked for keys before they go; one that looks like it holds one asks «send it anyway?». Images are
  never checked, so they never go to Telegram.

## Fewer messages (D-072)

- Telegram carries what needs you; the rest — plans ready, the operational PM's own updates, work finished — waits in
  the panel and the stand-up, which now also says what got done since yesterday.

## The team runs itself more

- **An operational PM (D-064):** a Paseo agent that follows the projects you hand it, answers you there and asks the
  agents for you; it records your tasks and tidies their duplicates.
- **Closed sessions are woken (D-074):** a message reopens a closed Paseo session, as when you write to it, instead of
  waiting in its inbox.
- **A loop guard (D-069)** warns, brakes and pauses agents going round in circles; **`/pause codex`** (D-075) holds every
  agent of a provider by hand, to save its credits.
- **The panel knows it is out of date** and offers a reload, keeping what you typed.

## Safety

- Redaction hides a secret's **value**, never its name or quotes, so commands like `grep '^X_TOKEN=' file` keep
  working; it now also catches camelCase names, keys in prose, `.env` forms, and keys by their own form (cloud, npm,
  Google, Stripe, GitLab, Slack, JWTs). Every pattern runs in linear time on untrusted text.
- Private projects never reach chat or the panel; yes is still matched by code; what an agent writes is untrusted.
